# Kanon

An esoteric movement of good taste, written in the agent layer.

## The canon

- The harness is the contract.
- A comment is a claim: it earns its place or it goes.
- The agent is an associate, not an oracle.
- The human sets the taste; the agent enforces the craft.
- Local-first: no registry, no policy engine.
- Excellence is proportional: small, fast, measured.
