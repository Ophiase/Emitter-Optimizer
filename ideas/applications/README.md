# Applications

**[sketch]** Every use of the engine we can imagine, one by one. The cases are
where the next idea comes from: each row asks the engine a question it may not
answer yet.

**The test for a row**: something spreads from a point, something wants it, and
the place of the point is the decision. If those three hold, it belongs here.

## How to read a row

- **Emitter**: what you place. **Wants it**: the density $\mu$. **Blocks**: $C$.
- **The idea**: what makes this row worth a thought; the rest is the same engine.
- **Needs** says what stands between the row and a run. It is our estimate, not
  a measurement, except for `Done`.

| Needs | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Done` | Simulated, with a [page](../../docs/cases/README.md) and numbers |
| `Laws` | Runs today: a map and two laws, nobody has tried it yet |
| `Objective` | A guarantee, a worst case, a budget or a harm in the gain ([objective](../model/objective.md)) |
| `Aim` | An emitter also has a direction ([physics](../model/physics.md)) |
| `Pairs` | Emitters act on each other directly ([circuits](../model/circuits.md)) |
| `Place` | An emitter may only stand somewhere ([constraints](../model/constraints.md)) |
| `Time` | The demand or the emitters change during the day |
| `Domain` | A space the kernel lacks: a road network, a graph, a mesh, many dimensions |

## What the catalogue teaches

Five turns come back in every family. They are the real inspiration here:

1. **Change the sign**: a thing that serves becomes a thing that bothers, and
   each place now loses (84 to 90).
2. **Swap the verbs**: to emit and to listen are the same geometry (91 to 99).
3. **Hide the answer**: what was a decision becomes a measurement to explain, as
   [urea](../model/urea.md) does (100 to 106).
4. **Replace the map**: time loops, tastes have distances, colours have a volume
   (107 to 123).
5. **Let the law carry the meaning**: fear, habit and effort all decay.

estimate: only someone to try them.

## What to simulate next

**[guess]** Our order: most surprise for the least code. All of them are `Laws`.

1. **Stippling a portrait** (115): an image becomes dots. The figure sells itself.
2. **Doses of a medicine** (107): a torus of 24 hours, and the map disappears.

## Left out on purpose

**[defined]** Nothing that aims at people: no weapons, no mass surveillance. The
engine would not notice the difference; this list does.

Every row but the `Done` ones is unchecked: the row is a thought, not a result.
A row becomes real with a figure, a baseline and its limits.
